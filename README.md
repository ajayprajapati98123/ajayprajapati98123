## 🚀 Featured Project

### 🌾 Agrikin &nbsp; ![SIH 2026](https://img.shields.io/badge/SIH_2026-Idea_Submission-0891b2?style=flat-square)

**Smart India Hackathon 2026** · Problem Statement SIH26193 · Team **Innov8x**

An AI-powered agricultural ecosystem that connects farmers to markets and gives them smart farming support in one place.

| Module | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🤝 **KrishiConnect** | Location-based matching of farmers with nearby buyers and sellers, with chat, image sharing and video calls |
| 🔍 **AI Detections** | Detects crop diseases, produce quality and soil health |
| 🌱 **Cropify AI** | Personalized crop and farming recommendations based on local conditions |
| ⛅ **Weather Intelligence** | Location-based weather updates and farming alerts |
| 🏛️ **Government Schemes** | Easy access to relevant agricultural schemes and benefits |
| 💧 **Smart Farming** | Modern irrigation, sustainable practices and AI-driven guidance |

<code>Next.js</code> <code>TypeScript</code> <code>Tailwind CSS</code> <code>OpenAI API</code> <code>WebRTC</code>
